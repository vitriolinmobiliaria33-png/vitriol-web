Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about our services. If you can't find what you are looking for, just write to us.

Properties

Renting

  • Usually an ID document, your last 3 payslips or proof of income, and a guarantor who owns property or a rent guarantee insurance policy (“seguro de caución”). Requirements may vary depending on the owner.

  • The deposit is usually one month's rent, at the first month's price. It is returned when the lease ends, once the condition of the property has been checked.

  • Rent is adjusted as agreed in the lease, using the indexes allowed by Argentine law. Your adviser will explain the terms before you sign.

Fees

  • For sales in Córdoba, Argentina, our standard fee is 3% for each party (buyer and seller), in line with the fee scale of the Córdoba Real Estate Brokers' Association (Colegio de Corredores Inmobiliarios de Córdoba). For properties in other markets, such as Spain's Costa del Sol, your adviser will tell you the fees for each transaction.
