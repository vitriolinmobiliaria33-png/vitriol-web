A country house surrounded by nature and silence, on a 6.1-hectare plot in Paso Blanco, 5 km from Agua de Oro on the road to La Cumbre. It has a main house and an independent guest house, its own spring water, solar power and around 130 fruit trees.

The houses

  • Main house of 140 m², including the covered verandas: 2 bedrooms, a 55 m² kitchen and living room, a bathroom with bathtub and a loft. Built of adobe brick with a quebracho, eucalyptus and colorado wood structure, a guayubira main beam and double roof insulation. Wooden windows and doors.
  • Independent guest house of 69 m², including the covered verandas, with a shower room and space for a small kitchen.

Energy and water

  • Solar power installation with a new inverter and lithium batteries (2026) in the main house
  • Solar water heater, with a back-up gas water heater for winter
  • A historic local spring that never runs dry, with an electric deep-well pump
  • Rainwater harvesting: 140,000 litres stored in 2 Australian tanks, with an electric pump for irrigation
  • 8 tanks of 1,000 litres for household use

The land

  • 6.1 hectares, with the perimeter fence renewed a year ago
  • About 130 fruit trees: peach, plum, walnut, pear, apple, fig, almond, quince, kiwi, pomegranate and a citrus tree
  • 6 raised vegetable beds
  • Carport for 2 vehicles with quebracho posts and a rustic bamboo-cane roof
  • Tool room, 5 woodsheds and another space with about 45 m³ of chopped firewood

Title deed, land registry and taxes up to date.

Offers considered. A property or a vehicle can be accepted as part payment.
