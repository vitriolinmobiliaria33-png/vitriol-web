A significant assembly of plots and warehouses on Bv. Las Heras, Pasaje Rivera Indarte and Pasaje Agustín Pérez, in one of the areas with the greatest development potential in the city, a short walk from the Suquía River promenade.

The site

  • Warehouse 1: 1,044 m² of land and 1,329 m² built. Site coverage (FOS) 80%
  • Former Capitán Blue corner: 243 m² of land and 49 m² built. Site coverage (FOS) 80%
  • Warehouse 2: 377 m² of land and 322 m² built. Site coverage (FOS) 80%

According to the zoning, up to ground floor plus 5 storeys can be built, with ground floor plus 1 on the street line of the larger plot. Our own estimate of the buildable envelope is about 6,700 m², to be confirmed with a preliminary design.

The sale includes
