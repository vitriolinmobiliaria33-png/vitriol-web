Buying property in Argentina as a foreigner

Foreign buyers can purchase property in Argentina; some rules apply to rural land and border areas. To sign the deed before a notary (escribano) you will need an Argentine tax ID (CDI). Property prices are usually quoted in US dollars. We guide you through the process step by step.
